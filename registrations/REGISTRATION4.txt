STUDY 4 - REGISTRATION. Approved by the author in chat on 2026-11-15 (21:27 Baghdad time) before any seed 400-409 was run.

1. Purpose
   Compare secret switching thresholds (Study 3) with the nearest prior idea and with two further baselines:
   a random switching DELAY (time-domain watermark in the style of Ahmed et al., 2024, arXiv:2409.16536),
   PARSIMONIOUS probing in the spirit of Naha et al. (2023, Automatica), and a passive reconstruction AUTOENCODER
   in the spirit of the BATADAL detectors (e.g. Raza et al., 2025). These are implementations "in the spirit of", not replications.

2. Design (fixed)
   Networks: C-Town, Richmond, Net6. Seeds 400-409 on each (never used). Development seeds 100-101 excluded.
   Arms, in this order: passive | ae | tog-rival (12 toggles/day, best single actuator) |
     pars-rival (base 4 toggles/day, 48/day while the probe statistic Q > 1.5; same actuator choice as tog-rival) |
     hyst0.3 (secret thresholds, dither 30 % of each band, inward) |
     delay0.3 (public thresholds; switch executed after a secret delay of 0..K steps; K matched per actuator to the 30 % dither from the
               level speed before a natural switch, clipped 1..12; safety cap: switch at once if the level is more than 30 % of the band
               beyond the threshold; detector additionally tests "switch withheld" events with a separate CUSUM, alarm budget split equally).
   Forger knowledge for delay0.3: knows the scheme and the delay range, not the drawn delay; sees commands one step late (same as for hyst0.3).
   False-alarm target 0.0005 per step (1/week) PRIMARY; 0.01 secondary. Calibration 56 days, independent clean run 56 days.
   8 episodes per scenario x forger; 12 h warm-up, 12 h attack. Forgers in model: twin, replay, observer-lag, zero-residual.
   Out of model (reported only): real-time observer. Scenarios are derived from the public-threshold model (first arm), as in Study 3.

3. Outcomes
   PRIMARY: per seed, mean placebo-corrected excess timeliness over scenarios and the four in-model forgers, at 1 false alarm/week.
   SAFETY: per seed, 99th percentile over the clean run of the largest distance of a controlled tank level outside its operating envelope
           (lowest on-threshold to highest off-threshold), as a fraction of the envelope (exc_p99).
   SECONDARY: worst-case excess; switch operations/day; realised false alarms/week; exc_frac, exc_max.

4. Hypotheses and tests (paired Wilcoxon over the 10 seeds, two-sided, alpha 0.05, per network)
   H4-1 (primary): hyst0.3 > delay0.3 on the primary outcome on each network.
   H4-2 (safety):  exc_p99 of hyst0.3 < exc_p99 of delay0.3 on each network.
   H4-3: the autoencoder does not detect: mean primary outcome of ae < 0.05 on each network.
   P4-1 (prediction): hyst0.3 > pars-rival on Richmond and Net6; pars-rival > hyst0.3 on C-Town.
   P4-2 (prediction): pars-rival >= tog-rival on each network.

5. Validity rules
   a. An episode in which the simulator fails or its clock does not advance is discarded and redrawn; counts reported per network.
   b. An arm whose realised false-alarm rate, averaged over the 10 seeds, exceeds 2 per week is reported as failing the budget and cannot
      support a hypothesis; the number of individual seeds above 2 is also reported. (Clarifies the ambiguity met in Study 3.)
   c. If switch operations/day of pars-rival exceed those of tog-rival by more than 15 %, pars-rival is reported as not cost-matched.
   d. Results are reported as obtained. No change to code, arms, outcomes or tests after freezing.

   the remaining gap on development seeds is mainly the number of switching events (delay lengthens pump cycles, inward dither shortens them).
