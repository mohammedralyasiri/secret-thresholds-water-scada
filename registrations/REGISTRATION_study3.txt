STUDY 3 - REGISTRATION (in force). Approved by the author in chat on 2026-11-15 before any seed 300-309 was run.

1. Question
   Does a secret-threshold (dithered hysteresis) defence, with toggle probes allocated to what natural switching leaves
   uncovered, detect forged tank levels better than toggle probes alone, at a realistic false-alarm rate?

2. Frozen material
   Code: run15/pilot15.py = run14dev/dev14d.py (matched event statistic, event CUSUM kappa 0.5, clip 5, simulator guard)
         plus per-arm resume files and a redraw of long attack-free runs when the simulator guard fires. No other change.
   Networks: C-Town, Richmond, Net6. Seeds 300-309 on each (never used before). Development seeds 100-101 excluded.
   Arms: passive | tog-rival (12 toggles/day, best single actuator) | tog-game (12/day, maximin) |
         hyst0.3 (secret thresholds, no toggles) | hyst0.3+ogame (secret thresholds + 12 toggles/day, offset game) [PROPOSED]
   Dither width fixed at 30 % of each operating band. Toggle budget fixed at 12/day.
   False-alarm target: 0.0005 per step (1 per week) PRIMARY; 0.01 per step secondary (continuity with Studies 1-2).
   Calibration 56 days, independent clean run 56 days. 8 episodes per scenario x forger; 12 h warm-up, 12 h attack.
   Forgers in model: twin, replay, observer-lag, zero-residual. Out of model (reported only): real-time observer.

3. Outcome
   Excess timeliness = timeliness under attack minus timeliness of the paired placebo episode (same seeds, no attack).
   PRIMARY: per seed, mean excess over scenarios and the four in-model forgers, at 1 false alarm/week.
   SECONDARY: per seed, minimum over scenario x forger of mean excess (worst case); switch operations per day; realised false alarms/week.

4. Hypotheses and tests (paired Wilcoxon over the 10 seeds, two-sided, Holm over the two comparisons per network)
   H1 (primary): hyst0.3+ogame > tog-rival AND > tog-game on the primary outcome, on each of the three networks.
       Supported on a network only if both Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 and both differences are positive.
   H2: hyst0.3 (no toggles) > the better of the two toggle arms on Richmond and Net6.
   Prediction P1: worst-case excess is below 0.15 for every arm on every network (no method gives coverage).
   Prediction P2: on C-Town, tog-rival > tog-game on the primary outcome (concentration beats the game when information is scarce).

5. Validity rules
   a. An episode in which the simulator clock does not advance is discarded and redrawn; the count is reported per network.
   b. An arm whose realised false-alarm rate exceeds 2 per week on the clean run is reported as failing the budget; its
      detection figures are shown but it cannot support a hypothesis.
   c. Results are reported as obtained. No change to detector, dither width, budget, outcome or tests after approval.

6. What is already known (development, 2 seeds, 4 episodes; NOT evidence)
   Primary outcome at 1 FA/week:   C-Town  Richmond  Net6
