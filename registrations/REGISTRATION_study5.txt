STUDY 5 (FROZEN) - Network-fidelity check of the secret-threshold watermark
=====================================================================================

Status: exploratory extension added after Study 4, at the authors' request, to answer the
question "does the result survive a real SCADA communication layer?". Not part of the
Study 3/4 registrations; it will be reported as a separate, clearly labelled study.

5a  Measurement (no hypothesis)
    Platform: DHALSIM (Murillo et al., 2023), C-Town topology, 9 PLCs + SCADA over
    EtherNet/IP on Mininet (Linux-bridge switches; no netem available), epynet simulator,
    5-min hydraulic step. PLC rule pairs replaced by secret-band controls (frac 0.3, redrawn
    after each switch; defender log of every switch with its secret threshold).
    Runs: normal operation, 2 simulated days (576 steps), seed 1. Warm-up: first 12 steps dropped.
    Outputs: for every tank tag, the fraction of steps on which the SCADA value differs from
    the ground-truth value of the same step and equals an earlier step ("stale"), the stale-run
    length distribution, and the lag between an actuator change in ground truth and in SCADA.

5b  Robustness study in the process-level simulator (registered hypothesis)
    Change to pilot16.py: the value reported to SCADA for each tank and each actuator status is
    replaced, independently per tag and step, by the previous reported value with the
    per-tag stale probability and run-length distribution measured in 5a (a two-state Markov
    channel fitted to 5a). The PLC itself is unaffected. Calibration and testing both see the
    channel. Nothing else changes (same KAPPA, ZCLIP, budgets, CAL 56, EP 8, forgers).
    Network: C-Town. Seeds 500-509. Arms: passive, hyst0.3, delay0.3.
    H5-1: hyst0.3 > delay0.3 on the primary outcome at 1 FA/week (paired Wilcoxon, p < 0.05).
    Also reported (no test): drop of hyst0.3's primary outcome relative to Study 4 C-Town,
    FA/week, redraws, exc metrics. Same validity rules as Study 4 (FA > 2/week invalidates).

5c  Demonstration on DHALSIM (descriptive only)
    One 2-day run with DHALSIM's built-in concealment MITM (payload replay of the tank-level tag
    to SCADA, as shipped in examples/ctown_topology) with secret-band PLCs. Report the switch
    log versus the SCADA-reported levels at the switch steps (figure), before and during the
    attack. No detection rate is claimed from a single run.
